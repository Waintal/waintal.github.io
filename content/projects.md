## Software

- **[Kwant](https://kwant-project.org)**: a Python package for quantum transport in tight-binding systems, developed with [Christoph Groth](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=9ZzfbNkAAAAJ) (CEA Grenoble), [Michael Wimmer](http://www.michaelwimmer.org) and [Anton Akhmerov](https://antonakhmerov.org) (TU Delft). A Kwant script looks very much like the equations one writes on the blackboard. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.2926) · [video abstract](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2eB9Nov3nns).
- **[Tkwant](https://tkwant.kwant-project.org)**: the time-dependent extension of Kwant, for simulating time-resolved quantum transport, developed with Thomas Kloss and Christoph Groth. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.03132).
- **[xfac and tensor4all](https://tensor4all.org)**: libraries for tensor cross interpolation and quantics tensor trains, developed within the tensor4all collaboration, notably with Yuriel Núñez Fernández, Marc K. Ritter, [Hiroshi Shinaoka](https://shinaoka.github.io) and [Jan von Delft](https://www.theorie.physik.uni-muenchen.de/lsvondelft/). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.02454).
- **KNIT** (historical): our first open-source quantum transport code, now superseded by Kwant. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/0711.3413) · [download release 1.2](/downloads/knit/KNITrelease1.2.tar.gz) (tar.gz, 5.5 MB) · [user guide](/downloads/knit/KNIT_user_guide.pdf) (pdf).

## Initiatives

- **[House of Quantum Alps](https://quantalps.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/maison-du-quantique-alpes/about-maison-du-quantique-alpes-1658769.kjsp)** (Maison du Quantique Alpes), of which I am currently the director. It brings together researchers and industrial partners around quantum computing and quantum-inspired methods, with a focus on applications.
- **[Intrica Computing](https://intricacomputing.com)**: a project, based in Geneva and supported by Innosuisse, that aims at bringing quantum-inspired (tensor network) methods to industrial applications. It is led by Julian Thoenniss; I am involved as co-founder and scientific advisor.
- **[SciPost](https://scipost.org)**: I am a fellow editor of this open-access journal.
